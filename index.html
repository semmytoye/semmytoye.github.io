About

Segun Emmanuel Ibitoye is a mechanical engineering researcher working at the intersection of sustainable energy, materials science, and applied machine learning. He serves as Director of Research & Development at the School of Technology, Woxsen University, Hyderabad, and holds a faculty position in the Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Ilorin, Nigeria.

He specializes in harnessing agricultural residues for sustainable energy production, notably designing and building a patented torrefaction reactor (NG/P/2022/196) and densification machine (NG/P/2022/194) for the generation of renewable and sustainable energy from wastes. These innovations serve as valuable resources for teaching and research, enabling the production of eco-friendly composite fuel briquettes and advancing waste-to-energy solutions that address global environmental challenges.

He is a reviewer for leading journals, including Bioresources and Bioprocessing, Bioenergy Research, Biomass Conversion and Biorefinery, Energy Conversion and Management, and Energy Reports. He has published extensively in reputable outlets and is a recipient of the CSIR-TWAS Fellowship and the 2025 Best Author Award from the Journal of Bioresources and Bioproducts.

Research programmes

Waste-to-Energy Valorization

Converting agricultural and municipal waste — rice husk, biomass residues — into biochar, solid fuel, and hydrogen-rich syngas through pyrolysis and steam reforming, modelled with Aspen Plus process simulation.

AI, IoT & Blockchain for Waste Systems

Reviewing how artificial intelligence, IoT sensing, and blockchain traceability can be combined to make waste classification, sorting, and circular-economy tracking smarter and more transparent.

Materials & Manufacturing Modelling

Characterising weld and surface-finish performance — hybrid flux welds, magnetic-assisted abrasive finishing — and building nature-inspired neural network models for micro additive manufacturing systems.

Swarm Robotics & Optimisation

Surveying particle swarm optimisation and rendezvous algorithms for swarm robotics, connecting bio-inspired search strategies to real coordination and control problems.

Computational Heat & Mass Transfer

Numerical studies of convective heat transfer in enclosures with rotating cylinders and double-diffusive buoyancy — foundational fluid dynamics work behind thermal system design.

Research Integrity & Authorship Ethics

Developing the Authorship Misappropriation Diamond framework to examine gift and diamond authorship practices, and the institutional pressures that normalise them.
